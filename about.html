01 / A short introduction

I’m Shaggy, an independent software developer based in Germany.

I build web products, developer tools, and experiments with a bias toward simple systems and thoughtful interfaces. My best work happens when product thinking and engineering share the same desk.

I work in public because progress is more credible when you can inspect it. The projects and activity across this site come directly from GitHub, including the quiet weeks and unfinished edges.

02 / How I work

A small set of
strong defaults.

The practical principles I bring to product decisions, code reviews, and the space between them.

01

Start with the actual problem.

Good software begins with clarity about who it is for and what should feel easier afterward.

02

Keep the system legible.

Names, boundaries, and boring decisions matter. Future work should be able to find its way around.

03

Ship a useful slice.

Momentum beats theatre. Put a coherent version in people’s hands, learn, and improve with evidence.
